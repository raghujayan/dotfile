
  HYPRLAND - the only four keys you need
  =====================================

    SUPER + M          QUIT.  Drops you at the login screen.
                       Click the gear, pick GNOME, and you are home.

    SUPER + Return     new terminal
    SUPER + Q          close the focused window
    SUPER + SHIFT + Q  close every window on this workspace
    SUPER + Space      app launcher (type a name, Enter)
    SUPER + H          this help

  Getting around
  --------------
    SUPER + arrows     move focus between windows
    SUPER + 1..5       switch workspace
    SUPER + SHIFT + 1..5   throw the window to that workspace
    SUPER + V          toggle floating for this window
    SUPER + SHIFT + V  clipboard history (pick with arrows, Enter)
    SUPER + F          fullscreen
    SUPER + SHIFT + F  cycle fan strategy (MODE in the top bar)

  Terminal and clipboard
  ----------------------
    CTRL + SHIFT + C   copy the selection
    CTRL + SHIFT + V   paste
    SUPER + SHIFT + V  anything copied earlier, no mouse needed

  The laptop keys work as printed: volume, brightness, mute.

  Windows tile themselves. Open a second one and the screen splits.
  There is no minimise - that is the point.

  Nothing here is permanent. GNOME is untouched and one SUPER+M away.
  This file: ~/.config/hypr/KEYS.txt
